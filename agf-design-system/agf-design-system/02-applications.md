# Applications

How the tokens leave the screen. Each division has a substrate calibration; the spine (brass, the three faces, the trace, the badge) is shared.

## The three divisions

| Division | Code | Theme | Substrate | Signature detail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Consumer | `AGF // HEAR` | Midnight, `surface-raised` forward | Forest floor anodise, walnut and leather accents | Knurled solid-brass volume crown; unlacquered, allowed to patina |
| Professional capture | `AGF // FIELD` | Midnight | Deepest-forest bead-blasted aluminium | Recessed photo-etched brass mark; pale-stone silkscreen |
| Broadcast | `AGF // AIR` | Air (OLED) | Obsidian anodised faceplates in `surface-sunken` cages | TiN-gold BNC and XLR collars; OLED telemetry on true black |

## CMF: colour, material, finish

Hand these to industrial designers and print houses in place of hex codes.

- **Deepest forest (`surface-deep`, #0E2317)**: 6061-T6 aluminium, bead-blasted, Type III hard-coat anodise matched to Pantone 5535 C. Chassis, sleeves, rack ears.
- **Forest floor (`forest`, #1F4A2D)**: Pantone 5535 C at 85% on 350 gsm uncoated cotton board for boxes; standard anodise for HEAR enclosures.
- **Obsidian (`obsidian`, #0E1612)**: black anodise with a green undertone (Pantone Black 6 C + 10% 5535 C) for AIR faceplates; OLED black for screens.
- **Brass (`brass`, #B8860B / `brass-glow`, #D4A234)**: machined C36000 free-cutting brass, unlacquered, for HEAR crowns, knobs, feet and gifts; PVD titanium nitride on stainless for FIELD and AIR connectors that must never tarnish; Pantone 871 C metallic or a satin brass hot foil in print, never process yellow.
- **Pale stone (`ink-on-deep-muted`, #C0B098)**: two-pass matte epoxy screen print on anodise, Pantone Warm Gray 3 C. All legends in `micro`, 2 mm cap height minimum, 8 mm grid.
- **Bone (`surface` in Daylight, #F3EEE4)**: natural uncoated 120 gsm stock for manuals and interiors; Pantone Warm Gray 1 C at 40%.

## Hardware

- Front plates follow the Faceplate component: mark left (etched, never printed), model in `strapline-small`, sections divided by engraved hairlines, legends in `micro`, 3 mm lamps that show only `brass-glow` (on) or `live` (clip, on air), brass-ringed jacks, chamfers that reveal raw brass like a trace line at the edge of a board.
- Knobs follow the Knob component: anodised forest caps, a brass pointer line, twenty-one ticks over 270°, a specular rim where the chamfer catches light.
- **The ground-lift switch** is the signature interaction on every product. All other toggles are matte black; the ground-lift is solid brass with FLOAT engraved in Poiret One beside it and the Floating Ground badge etched above.
- Rear panels and the inside of lids carry the trace field at one screen-print weight. Front plates never do.
- Embedded displays run the Air theme: `obsidian` ground, grid lines in `surface-raised` at 10 dB intervals, the frequency curve as a 2px `brass-glow` stroke with a 25%-to-transparent fill beneath, meters as the Meter component in the three signal tokens, a vectorscope drawn in `signal-nominal` inside the orbital rings.

## Packaging and the box

- The outer box is forest-floor cotton board with the trace field blind-debossed at 0.3 mm; only the mark is hot-foiled in satin brass (the Sleeve component is the reference). Interiors are Daylight: bone stock, `ink` text, one trace rule, no pattern.
- The **calibration certificate** (Certificate component) rests on top of the unit: a black-core card with gilded edges, "Measured in silence." in Poiret One, this unit's measurements, the engineer's name. It is the same document on the web when the box's NFC tag is tapped.
- Order of reveal: sleeve, certificate, cloth-wrapped unit, cables coiled in bone paper, the Floating Ground badge last, printed inside the base.

## Gifts

The Seal and the badge carry gifts; the full lockup does not.

- **FR4 coaster and business card**: a real 1.6 mm PCB, matte forest solder mask, ENIG gold traces forming the type and a trace-field margin, an NFC coil inside the orbital ring of the badge that opens the spec library.
- **Espresso cup**: matte deep-forest porcelain, a glazed brass rim, warm-stone interior, the seal debossed under the foot.
- **Turntable weight / desk totem**: 450 g brushed C36000 brass with the mark milled in relief into the top face, forest leather base pad, serial engraved on the side in `micro`.
- **Notebook, leather goods**: the seal blind-debossed, no foil, no copy beyond the strapline on the flyleaf.

## Web

- The site runs Midnight by default with Daylight sections for long reading (journal, manuals) and never more than two theme changes a page.
- Page spine: Nav, Hero, Heading stacks over `surface`, feature cards on `surface-raised`, a Readout row, a SpecTable, a `surface-deep` footer carrying the badge and the lockup.
- Photography is desaturated toward forest and warm stone, lit from one side so brass reads as metal; it always sits under a `scrim` where text crosses it, with grain over it and no trace field.
- Breakpoints keep the type scale; the hero headline steps 96 → 72 → 48. The trace field drops to `opacity-trace` × 0.6 under 640px.
