# Cover

The brand cover, a 960 × 288 banner (10:3) for the showcase, decks, social headers and the first page of the brand book: a `surface` ground in the Midnight theme (wrap it in `data-theme="midnight"`) with the name set bottom-left at `space-48` from the edges, "All Grounds Floating" on two lines in Poiret One at 72px, `ink`, with "Pure signal." beneath it in Commissioner `small`, `ink-muted`. The art fills the right half, about 38% of the area, and is built from four blocks with `radius-4` corners and `space-16` gutters: a tall `brass` slab bled off the top (the conductor, the largest block), a `forest` field beside it bled off the right and bottom (the substrate), a pale `ink-on-deep-muted` block under the brass bled off the bottom (the silkscreen), and one 48 × 16 `signal-clip` block. Over the blocks run three groups of four parallel 1px traces at a `space-8` pitch, as Monoton's four strokes do: brass over the forest, and `surface`-coloured cut-outs in the brass slab. Each group bends once at 45° and ends in a 6px via. Sides are multiples of `space-8` and `space-16`, and nothing in the art sits within 32px of the name. The consumer provides the container and the theme wrapper; the artwork and the two text lines are fixed. It must not carry a photograph, the TraceField tile or any second pattern; use `signal-clip` once and only for that block, since it means clip or on air; take no gradient, shadow or glow; bend traces at no angle but 45°; set the name in Monoton; or rewrite the strapline or the tagline.
