# Cover

The brand cover, a 960 × 288 banner (10:3) for the showcase, decks, social headers and the first page of the brand book: a `surface-deep` band wrapped in `data-theme="midnight"` (the always-dark band, so every token inside resolves dark), carrying the TraceField on its left half only (behind the words, never under the art) and grain at `opacity-grain`. The words sit bottom-left at `space-48` from the edges as a stack: the mark, the letters AGF in Monoton at `mark-mega` (the `agf-wordmark agf-wordmark--lg` mark, brass, without its built-in strap); then, `space-16` below, the strapline "All Grounds Floating" in Poiret One at `strapline` (24px), uppercase and tracked 0.3em, in `ink-on-deep-muted`, which is the page's one Poiret One line; then, `space-16` below that, the headline "Pure signal." in Commissioner 200 at 40px, `ink`. The art fills the right half, about 38% of the area, and is built from four blocks with `radius-4` corners and `space-16` gutters: a tall `brass` slab bled off the top (the conductor, the largest block), a `forest` field beside it bled off the right and bottom (the substrate), a pale `ink-on-deep-muted` block under the brass bled off the bottom (the silkscreen), and one 48 × 16 `signal-clip` block. Over the blocks run three groups of four parallel 1px traces at a `space-8` pitch, as Monoton's four strokes do: brass over the forest, and `surface-deep`-coloured cut-outs in the brass slab. Each group bends once at 45° and ends in a 6px via. Sides are multiples of `space-8` and `space-16`, and nothing in the art sits within 32px of the words. The consumer provides the container; the band carries its own `data-theme="midnight"`, and the artwork, the mark, the strapline and the headline are fixed. It must not carry a photograph, a trace field under the art or any second pattern; use `signal-clip` once and only for that block, since it means clip or on air; take no gradient, shadow or glow; bend traces at no angle but 45°; set any word but AGF in Monoton; set the strapline lower case, untracked or larger than `strapline-hero` (40px); put a second Poiret One line on the cover; or rewrite the strapline or the tagline.
