# Rule

Dividers at the three drafting weights: `border-hairline` in `brass-hairline` between list items, table rows and cards; `border-rule` in `brass` under a section title or as the 48px short rule in the heading stack; `border-bold` in `brass` to open a chapter or stand beside a quotation. The **trace** variant ends in two solder pads and is the only decorated rule; use it where a section hands off to the next, never more than once a screen. Rules are full-bleed within their container or exactly `space-48` wide; nothing in between.
