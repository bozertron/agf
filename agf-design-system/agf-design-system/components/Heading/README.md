# Heading

The standard headline stack: an `eyebrow` (uppercase Commissioner, `ink-faint` or `brass-text`), a `display` headline in `ink` at weight 300, a 48px `rule` in `brass`, then a `lede` in `ink-muted` at weight 300. Headlines are sentence case with a full stop when they are a sentence; they break at the thought, not at the measure. Keep ledes under 60 characters a line. For the most important statement on a page swap the eyebrow for `strapline-small` in Poiret One, and do that once per page at most; the strapline is the reserved voice.
