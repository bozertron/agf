The family line

AGF

Three marks, one language: signal drawn as parallel lines. AGF is the masterbrand in Monoton; Globl Radio is the live service, five lines in five rings; stereOS is the operating system, five lines on every letter and the ring for its O. On a shared baseline the stereOS cap height equals the AGF cap height and the GR ring stands 2.6 cap heights tall.

Editions

// EMBEDDED

stereOS embedded

The RTOS. Sound-first devices with no screen to speak of: over-ear headphones, the earbud case, the dock. Boots to the OS monogram on an OLED; the ring is etched beside the Floating Ground badge.

// MOBILE

stereOS mobile

Linux. Licensed in by vehicle makers: cars, boats, off-road, and the high-end boom box. The adopter's badge reads runs stereOS; the adopter's own brand stays on the dash.

// HOME

stereOS Home

Linux, the flagship. Not a desktop: the guarantee that the box running the house is a world-class sound system. The Instrument, the Core-likes, the Symphony's electronics. Runs the Air theme on its screens.

// PRO

stereOS Pro

Standard in all AGF broadcast equipment and the Balcony. The capture end of acoustic teleportation: a Pro device at the venue, Globl Radio in between, a Home or embedded device at the couch.
