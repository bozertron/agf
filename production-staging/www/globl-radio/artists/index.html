The stereo master is yours. The standard stereo capture and the on-site recording belong to the artist, full stop: mix it, master it, sell it through any channel. AGF takes nothing.

The broadcast mix is shared 50/50, in perpetuity. The broadcast-mixed version, the one that reaches the global audience live, is co-owned, and the split never changes.

You can say no. The artist keeps the right to block any subsequent broadcast by the venue, without cause, even on the 50/50 version.

You set the price. Virtual tickets carry an artist-set baseline, and the fan’s forever copy is priced by you.

If you perform, and you want the room you play to reach everyone who could not be in it, we would like to talk.
