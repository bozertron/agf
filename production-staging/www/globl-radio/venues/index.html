What a venue gains.

A second room without walls. Every performance in the house can be sold again as a virtual ticket, at a quality that makes the stream worth paying for, with the venue and the artist sharing the proceeds.

The Balcony. State-of-the-art capture and production hardware installed in the house: the inaugural home of AGF’s l’Art Production line, with royalty-paid integrations from the live-mixing techs who use it.

A reason to come in. The Front of House stays what it is, a stage and a bar, with ticket donations going in full to local artists. Nothing on the walls sells anything.

One venue per market. AGF works with one flagship venue in each financially qualifying market, chosen for the density of arts-aware listeners, the strength of the local performing-arts ecosystem and an owner-operator willing to host the Front of House, Balcony and Green Room blueprint.
