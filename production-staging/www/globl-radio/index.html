Globl Radio

Be there.

At any given time, there are talented artists delighting audiences.

Artists and performance venues create partnerships to provide audiences memorable experiences.

Globl Radio believes these experiences, bound by geography and physical limitations, have the potential to generate immense economic and social value if the care was taken to present them to global audiences at a ‘you are there’ level of quality.

AGF engineered the Globl Radio experience as one product that accounts for capturing the performance at a venue, shipping it to your home, and then accounting for your home’s environment so it’s presented while it’s happening, at a level of quality that’s unprecedented.
