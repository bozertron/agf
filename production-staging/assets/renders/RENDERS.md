## symphony-macro-system - The Symphony Macro-System

18 images, from two separate render sets of the same tower (see below).

Two render sets of the same tower sit side by side in this folder; the first 11 rows are set A and the last 7 are set B. **Set A** (11 JPGs, numbered `-60` to `-102`, 1920x1083 each) comes from the old `product-renders/` folder: shaded renders on a flat mauve-grey studio background, covering black with brass trim (full tower plus close-up), black with a brass woofer ring and no trim (two full-tower angles plus close-up), a black tower with both brass trim and a brass ring (full tower only), copper (woofer close-up only, no full tower), forest green with copper ring and trim (full tower plus close-up) and walnut (full tower plus close-up). **Set B** (7 PNGs, no numbers in the names) comes from screenshots saved from the old site (original names `Screenshot From 2026-06-15 12-24-33.png` to `12-39-36.png`): 1116x807 for the full-tower views, 1116x825 or 994x845 for the close-ups, with the same flat mauve-grey background (RGB 119,108,106) and the same tower shape. It covers walnut (front plus driver close-up), copper (front plus driver close-up), green with brass (front plus driver close-up) and black with a brass ring (driver close-up only, no full tower). Set B is lower resolution than set A. The PNGs are RGBA files but every pixel is fully opaque (alpha 255 throughout), so they are **not** transparent-background cut-outs, despite earlier notes saying so. The finishes overlap but do not match one for one: black and green/brass-or-copper appear in both with different camera angles and slightly different ring tones (set A green is named copper, set B green is named brass), walnut is in both, and copper has a full tower only in set B. Nothing in the files says which set is the more current or authoritative one.
